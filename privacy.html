Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Introduction

ChineseGo ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Data Collection

ChineseGo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from users.

The App operates entirely offline for its core functionality. We do not:

  • Collect personal information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, photos, or other personal data
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Store any user-generated content on our servers

In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. We do not have access to:

  • Your payment information (credit card, PayPal, etc.)
  • Your Apple ID or account details
  • Purchase history (beyond what Apple provides for transaction verification)

Apple handles all payment processing and data collection related to purchases. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information about how Apple handles your data.

Concierge Service

If you use our Concierge service (real human translation support via phone or message), you will be contacting our support team directly. This is not processed through the App. We do not record or store phone call content.

Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Your language preference
  • Your theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Your font size preference
  • Your favorite phrases
  • Your learning progress
  • Your subscription status (for purchased content)

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple StoreKit — for processing in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, or any other third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

ChineseGo is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, this is not applicable.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your personal information through our App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
