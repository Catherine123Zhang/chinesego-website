Common Topics

📥

How to restore purchases

Open the app → Settings → Tap "Restore Purchases". Your previous purchases will be restored across all your devices.

📴

Offline mode

All phrases and audio work 100% offline after download. No internet connection is ever needed for the core features.

🔄

How to request a refund

All purchases are processed by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund within Apple's refund window.

🌐

Changing language

Open the app → Settings → select your preferred language from the 11 available options. The entire UI and translations will update.

🆘

Concierge service

Need real human translation help during your trip? Contact us via email or messaging to arrange concierge support.

Note: All subscriptions and purchases are processed via Apple In-App Purchase. We provide messaging contact info for customer support only — not for processing payments.
